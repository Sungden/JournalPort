# Evidence and Licensing Policy

Public accessibility does not imply permission to redistribute. JournalPort stores only what is
needed to audit a rule.

## May be stored

- Original JournalPort profile metadata, rule structures, URLs, retrieval timestamps, locators,
  hashes, and contributor-authored interpretations.
- Short, necessary evidence extracts when quotation is legally appropriate, with owner/source
  attribution. The schema caps extracts at 500 characters; contributors should use less.
- Original synthetic or clearly licensed fixtures, with license recorded.

## Link/hash only by default

- Full journal/publisher web pages, PDFs, submission-portal pages, author instructions, and policy
  documents unless redistribution permission is explicit.
- Paywalled, account-only, personalized, or portal-generated material.
- Page assets, screenshots, logos, branding, and publisher CSS/scripts.

Hashes describe the retrieved content but convey no redistribution right. A hash plus URL,
timestamp, title, owner, locator, and minimal evidence extract is the default snapshot.

## Templates and assets

Official Word/LaTeX templates, checklists, CSL files, logos, and sample artwork remain external
unless their license permits repository redistribution. Record URL, version/hash, and license;
never relabel them Apache-2.0. Derived factual rule data must not reproduce expressive content
unnecessarily.

## Review

Every profile contribution must identify source owner and type, retrieval time, snapshot hash,
minimal evidence, and storage basis. Unclear rights mean link/hash only. Takedown requests should
remove stored extracts without erasing the historical hash/metadata record where lawful.
