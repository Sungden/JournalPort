# Third-party notices and data boundaries

JournalPort original code and documentation are Apache-2.0. Python dependencies retain their own
licenses. Journal names and short guideline evidence excerpts identify factual sources and remain
the property of their respective owners; inclusion does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

The repository stores URLs, hashes, metadata, locators, and limited excerpts needed to audit rules.
It does not intentionally redistribute complete publisher pages, official templates, logos, CSL
assets, or private manuscripts. Test manuscripts and generated binary fixtures are synthetic.
Contributors must document a reusable/open license for any future third-party fixture or keep only
link/hash metadata when rights are unclear.
